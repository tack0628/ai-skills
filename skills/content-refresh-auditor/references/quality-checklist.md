# Quality checklist

Run this check before delivering an audit. Fix failures rather than merely listing them.

## Source integrity

- [ ] Every analyzed article maps to supplied content and a stable URL or ID, or the mapping limitation is explicit.
- [ ] Sources, date ranges, filters, dimensions, and comparison periods are named.
- [ ] Partial periods, new pages, seasonal pages, migrations, and inconsistent filters are handled or flagged.
- [ ] No article text, metric, query, trend, intent, date, competitor, or business value was invented.
- [ ] Observed facts, calculations, interpretations, user policy, and unknowns remain distinct.

## Diagnosis

- [ ] Demand, visibility, CTR, freshness, intent, quality, overlap, and strategic value were not collapsed into one unexplained score.
- [ ] Click decline was not treated as demand decline without impression evidence.
- [ ] Aggregate average position and CTR were not treated as exact or universal measures.
- [ ] CTR comparisons account for position/query/device mix when the data permits, and limitations are stated when it does not.
- [ ] Ranking decline was not used by itself to justify REFRESH.
- [ ] Topic similarity was not used by itself to claim cannibalization.
- [ ] Update age was not used by itself to prove staleness or freshness.
- [ ] Material alternative explanations and counterevidence were considered.

## Action quality

- [ ] Each article has one clear primary action; any secondary action expresses a real sequence or gate.
- [ ] The intervention is the smallest one supported by the evidence.
- [ ] Every recommendation includes evidence, confidence, missing evidence, bounded scope, and a concrete next step.
- [ ] KEEP includes a monitoring or reassessment trigger.
- [ ] TUNE and REFRESH identify what changes and what remains.
- [ ] MERGE names examined candidates and preserves unique value without inventing a destination URL.
- [ ] SPLIT identifies distinct intents or audiences and explains why simpler restructuring is insufficient.
- [ ] PRUNE is advisory, includes pre-action checks, and requires human approval.
- [ ] HUMAN identifies a specific decision and the person or evidence needed; it is not a vague fallback.

## Human authorship and safety

- [ ] First-hand experience, opinion, interviews, sensitive claims, and distinctive voice are marked for preservation or human review.
- [ ] Legal, medical, financial, rights, privacy, compliance, and brand-sensitive decisions are escalated appropriately.
- [ ] Destructive or externally visible actions are not implied by the audit.

## Portfolio and handoff

- [ ] Priority reflects impact, urgency, confidence, and effort rather than weakness alone.
- [ ] Sitewide conclusions are supported by more than one isolated example.
- [ ] Handoffs to drafting, quality review, or internal-link work include the evidence and constraints needed by the related skill.
- [ ] User feedback is stored separately from evidence and recommendation history.
- [ ] No persistent preference is recorded without explicit authorization, scope, provenance, and exceptions.
