# Quality checklist

Run this before delivering the research package.

## Scope and coverage

- [ ] Topic, purpose, scope, exclusions, currentness needs, and downstream use are explicit.
- [ ] Core questions are answerable, prioritized, and each is answered, partial, or unresolved.
- [ ] The package is proportional to the request and does not claim exhaustive coverage.

## Source quality

- [ ] Primary and authoritative sources were sought before relying on secondary summaries.
- [ ] Every retained source was inspected beyond a search snippet when accessible.
- [ ] Each source is classified for the specific claim it supports, with authority, independence, currency, and limitations considered.
- [ ] Secondary sources are used for discovery, context, explanation, or an explicitly disclosed fallback.
- [ ] Repeated reports derived from one upstream source are not counted as independent confirmation.
- [ ] Missing primary evidence is stated without treating `not found` as `does not exist`.

## Claim integrity

- [ ] Each substantive conclusion is atomic and labeled `verified fact`, `reported claim`, `inference`, `opinion`, or `unresolved`.
- [ ] Confidence matches evidence strength and is not inflated by source count alone.
- [ ] Claims preserve source scope, definitions, time range, jurisdiction, version, and qualifications.
- [ ] Conflicts, counterevidence, inaccessible evidence, and material uncertainty are visible.
- [ ] No quotations, facts, dates, authors, identifiers, study details, URLs, or provenance were invented.

## Provenance and handoff

- [ ] `Q`, `C`, and `S` IDs are unique and cross-references resolve.
- [ ] Each source has a canonical URL or persistent identifier, relevant dates, access date, supported claims, important points, and limitations.
- [ ] Every executive finding maps to claim and source IDs.
- [ ] Evidence gaps include useful search coverage and the best next check.
- [ ] Downstream notes distinguish established facts, attributed claims, inference or opinion, and do-not-use-yet items.
- [ ] Freshness triggers are recorded for time-sensitive claims.

## Output boundary

- [ ] The deliverable is a research package, not a finished article or unsupported verdict.
- [ ] Quotations are minimal and necessary; paraphrases preserve meaning.
- [ ] The stable output headings and machine-handoff fields are retained unless the user requested another structured format.
