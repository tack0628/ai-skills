# Action framework

Choose the smallest action supported by the diagnosis. Assign one primary action. Use a secondary action only for a real dependency or implementation sequence.

## KEEP — preserve useful coexistence

Use when pages have distinct search intent, audience, specificity, funnel stage, or site role; when one page is stably primary and secondary exposure is not harmful; when combined outcomes are healthy; or when intervention has no evidenced benefit.

State the distinct role of every page, any architecture signal worth preserving, and a monitoring trigger. KEEP is a positive conclusion, not “no work because data is missing.”

## DIFFERENTIATE — clarify separate purposes

Use when pages should remain separate but their promise, targeting, title, headings, scope, or overlapping sections blur their roles. Define:

- Primary intent and audience for each page
- Content to retain, remove, relocate, or expand
- Title/heading/intro or structural boundary to clarify
- Query or page-role ownership to monitor
- Risk of over-optimization or loss of useful material

Do not prescribe cosmetic keyword substitutions when the underlying intent remains duplicated.

## MERGE — consolidate overlapping value

Use when examined pages substantially serve the same intent, separate pages add little durable value, and a unified resource is likely clearer for readers and site architecture. Identify all candidate pages and propose the retained purpose; do not invent the destination URL.

Before execution, inventory unique facts, firsthand material, media, conversions, backlinks, internal links, rankings, compliance needs, and page-specific traffic. MERGE is an editorial decision and content plan, not authorization to remove URLs.

## REDIRECT — implement an approved URL retirement or migration

REDIRECT is normally a secondary action after an approved MERGE, retirement, or migration. It is not a diagnosis and should not be recommended simply because two pages overlap.

Require:

- Verified source URL and approved, intent-equivalent destination
- Confirmation that valuable source content has been preserved where appropriate
- Canonical/indexation and redirect-chain review
- Update plan for important internal links, sitemap, and navigation
- Checks for conversions, backlinks, legal/compliance needs, and unintended losses
- Human approval and post-change monitoring

If the correct destination or consequences are unclear, use HUMAN rather than guessing.

## INTERNAL-LINK — make hierarchy and ownership explicit

Use when pages legitimately coexist but weak or ambiguous internal architecture obscures a parent/child, hub/detail, overview/deep-dive, series, or primary/supporting relationship. Specify the relationship and hand the stable inventory to `$internal-link-architect` for exact link placement and anchors.

Internal linking is not a universal cure for equivalent duplicate intent. If pages truly promise the same outcome, first decide whether to differentiate or merge.

## HUMAN — require a bounded decision

Use when evidence is insufficient for the requested consequence or the choice depends on editorial strategy, unpublished business value, sensitive claims, firsthand authorship, rights, conversions, backlinks, canonical/redirect risk, or unresolved source conflicts.

Name the exact question, decision owner when known, and evidence needed. Examples include choosing the authoritative destination, deciding whether two audiences are intentionally separate, or verifying which page converts. HUMAN is not a vague substitute for analyzing available evidence.

## Selecting among actions

| Diagnosis | Usually consider | Important gate |
|---|---|---|
| Distinct roles with useful overlap | KEEP; optionally INTERNAL-LINK | Document each role and monitor |
| Separate pages are justified but promises blur | DIFFERENTIATE; optionally INTERNAL-LINK | Define intent ownership before editing |
| Equivalent intent with little unique value | MERGE; then possibly REDIRECT | Preserve value and obtain human approval |
| Architecture ambiguity without equivalent intent | INTERNAL-LINK | Stable page roles and verified inventory |
| Content-only overlap screening | HUMAN, KEEP with monitoring, or bounded DIFFERENTIATE proposal | Do not claim measured cannibalization |
| Conflicting, sparse, or unreliable evidence | HUMAN | State the exact missing or conflicting evidence |

Action and finding labels are independent. A likely competition finding can still lead to DIFFERENTIATE rather than MERGE; useful coexistence can lead to KEEP plus INTERNAL-LINK; a competition candidate can lead to a data-collection plan rather than content changes.

## Success and reassessment

Match success signals to the action and available baseline:

- Clearer, more stable intended query ownership across complete comparable periods
- Stable or improved combined clicks/impressions for the query cluster, interpreted alongside demand and position
- Preserved conversions, backlinks, unique content, and important internal pathways
- Reduced content redundancy with clearer fulfillment of each page's promise
- No redirect errors, chains, loops, or unexpected indexing outcomes after an approved implementation

Do not promise ranking gains. Define a measurement window appropriate to recrawl, seasonality, query volume, and the user's reporting cadence, or state that the window cannot yet be determined.
