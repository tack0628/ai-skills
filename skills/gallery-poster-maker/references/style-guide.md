# Gallery Poster Maker — Style Guide

## Source brief

Each uploaded photo becomes one independent premium minimalist poster.

Never combine multiple source images into one collage unless the user explicitly asks for that.

## Canvas

- Orientation: vertical
- Aspect ratio: 3:4
- Layout split: exactly 50% upper / 50% lower
- Overall balance: calm, restrained, centered, spacious

## Upper half — photographic treatment

Keep the original image recognizable and structurally intact.

Preserve:
- subject geometry;
- authentic texture;
- natural lighting;
- natural shadows;
- the source palette and atmosphere.

Only subtle professional grading is allowed.

Desired references:
- editorial photography;
- art-exhibition presentation;
- architectural photography;
- high-end design publications.

When necessary, extend surrounding environmental content naturally, such as:
- ceiling;
- floor;
- sky;
- wall;
- landscape;
- ambient background.

Never stretch or deform the subject.

## Lower half — abstract interpretation

Identify the most distinctive visual structure of the main subject.

Translate it into:
- minimal geometric shapes;
- flat planes of color;
- fine line work;
- negative space.

Avoid:
- photorealistic rendering;
- painterly illustration;
- excessive detail;
- decorative complexity.

The abstraction should still be immediately traceable to the original subject.

Use colors sampled or inferred from the upper photographic region.

Optional abstract environmental devices:
- thin horizontal rules;
- thin vertical rules;
- sparse grids;
- simplified horizon lines;
- architectural framing;
- minimal spatial markers.

## Background and spacing

Prefer:
- beige;
- warm off-white;
- pale neutral tones;
- muted colors.

Use large areas of breathing room.

Center or optically balance the main visual mass.

Avoid cramped layouts.

## Typography

Use only a small amount of type.

Suitable elements:
- short English title;
- year;
- small series name;
- minimal classification text.

Typography should feel editorial, architectural, gallery-like, and premium.

Avoid oversized marketing copy, slogans, badges, stickers, or busy metadata blocks.

## Aesthetic references

The poster should feel influenced by:
- international design studios;
- architectural posters;
- contemporary art exhibitions;
- luxury brand visual systems;
- premium editorial design.

The final result should communicate:
- refinement;
- modernity;
- quietness;
- artistic restraint;
- intentional composition.

## Avoid

Do not introduce:
- generic glossy reflections;
- cheap template aesthetics;
- cartoon motifs;
- chibi or anime styling;
- heavy digital effects;
- neon/cyberpunk treatment;
- fake 3D mockup effects;
- unnecessary gradients;
- clutter;
- visual noise;
- excessive ornaments.

## Quality check

Before finalizing, verify:
1. The poster is 3:4 vertical.
2. Upper and lower halves are visually 1:1.
3. The source subject is not distorted.
4. The lower half is abstract, not realistic.
5. The source remains recognizable.
6. The palette feels connected across both halves.
7. Negative space is generous.
8. Text is minimal.
9. The result feels premium rather than templated.
10. Each source image has its own standalone poster.
