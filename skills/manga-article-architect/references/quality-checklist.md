# Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

1. Every plot claim is supported by the supplied material.
2. No dialogue has been invented.
3. No speculation is presented as canon.
4. Events appear in a readable order.
5. Headings describe actual content rather than generic hype.
6. The article does not repeat the same information unnecessarily.
7. Recap and analysis are clearly separated.
8. Analysis explains why a detail matters.
9. The article is not padded to reach a target length.
10. Character names, chapter number, techniques, and terminology are internally consistent.
11. The conclusion adds a useful synthesis instead of repeating the lead verbatim.
12. The meta description, if included, accurately reflects the article.
13. Copyrighted dialogue or source text has not been reproduced excessively.
