# Article Structure

Use this as the default structure when the user does not specify another format.

## Title

Generate 3 to 5 natural title candidates.

A strong title should normally contain:
- manga title;
- chapter number;
- the chapter's strongest hook, reveal, conflict, or unusual mechanic.

Avoid clickbait that overstates the supplied facts.

## Lead

Write 2 to 4 short paragraphs.

The lead should:
- identify the chapter;
- summarize what makes it notable;
- mention the main conflict or reveal;
- create interest without repeating the entire recap.

## H2: ネタバレ

Use approximately 3 to 6 H3 sections when the material supports them.

Typical shapes:

### プロローグ
Use when the chapter opens with setup, transition, or scene-setting.

### [Main event / scene]
Name the section after the actual development.

### [Second major event / reveal]
Continue in readable chronological order.

### エピローグ
Use when the chapter ends on a transition, reveal, cliffhanger, or aftermath.

Do not force "プロローグ" or "エピローグ" if they do not fit the chapter.

## H2: 感想・考察

Separate recap from interpretation.

Potential H3 themes:
- 特に気になった点
- Character decision or motivation
- Battle mechanic or supernatural rule
- Foreshadowing
- Thematic implications
- What the ending may mean
- 次回の展開予想

Only include sections that have meaningful content.

## H2: まとめ

Summarize:
- the chapter's central development;
- the most important implication;
- what remains unresolved.

Keep it concise.

## Meta description

When requested, write a natural search-result description.

Prefer approximately 90 to 140 Japanese characters when practical, but prioritize clarity over exact character counts.

Include:
- manga title;
- chapter number;
- major chapter hook;
- recap / impressions / analysis intent where natural.
